## Who they are

Carl Icahn, born in 1936, is one of the best-known activist investors in the United States. He started his own brokerage, Icahn & Co., in 1968 and drew national attention in the 1980s with takeover battles including Trans World Airlines and Texaco. Later campaigns at RJR Nabisco, eBay, Apple and Herbalife made him a regular presence in boardroom fights.

His main public vehicle is Icahn Enterprises L.P. (IEP), a Nasdaq-listed diversified holding company with interests in energy, automotive, food packaging, real estate and other businesses, plus investment funds run alongside Icahn's personal capital. IEP has faced pressure since a short-seller report by Hindenburg Research in May 2023, after which the partnership halved its quarterly distribution from $2 to $1 per unit. In August 2024, the SEC said IEP and Icahn had not properly disclosed pledges of IEP units backing personal margin loans; they settled for civil penalties of $1.5 million and $500,000, respectively, without admitting or denying the findings.

## How they invest

Icahn's approach combines deep-value buying with direct pressure on management: he builds a large stake, often files a Schedule 13D, and then seeks board seats, asset sales, spin-offs, buybacks or a sale of the company. He is comfortable holding controlling stakes for many years. CVR Energy, a refiner majority-owned through IEP, is the clearest current example. The investment funds also use hedges and short positions, which do not appear in a 13F. In the third quarter of 2026, IEP reported $522 million of losses on refining hedges held in those funds.

## Latest disclosed moves

Icahn's 13F for the quarter ended 30 September 2026 was filed on 13 November 2026. It reports about $9.43 billion across ten US-listed positions, with no new holdings.

- **Top holdings:** IEP about 47% (about 658.9 million units), CVR Energy about 39% (about 71.2 million shares), CVR Partners about 5.5%, Centuri about 3%, IFF about 2.7%.
- **Added:** IEP, up about 40.5 million units; SandRidge Energy, a small increase.
- **Trimmed:** International Flavors & Fragrances (about -30%), EchoStar (about -64%), Bausch + Lomb (about -32%).
- **Exited:** JetBlue Airways and American Electric Power.
- **Other news:** IEP reported a third-quarter net loss attributable to unitholders of $225 million. It said indicative net asset value rose to about $3.47 billion, largely from a gain on its CVR Energy stake, and it declared a $0.50 distribution. CVR Energy has agreed to buy two refineries from Marathon Petroleum for $4.2 billion.

Icahn has been adding IEP units by taking distributions in units rather than cash. One Form 4 shows 36.5 million units received in June 2026.

## How to follow them quickly

- **Form 13F:** quarterly, up to 45 days after quarter-end, filed under Carl C. Icahn, CIK 0000921669 ([EDGAR](https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=0000921669&type=&dateb=&owner=include&count=40)).
- **Schedule 13D:** the key disclosure for an activist. Since 2024 it is due within five business days of crossing 5%, and amendments follow material changes.
- **Form 4:** within two business days for trades by insiders and holders of more than 10%, including Icahn's dealings in IEP, CVR Energy and CVR Partners.
- **IEP filings:** quarterly results, 10-Q/10-K reports and indicative NAV updates under CIK 0000813762 ([EDGAR](https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=0000813762&type=&dateb=&owner=include&count=40)), plus open letters and press releases during campaigns.

## What the filings don't show

The 13F is up to 45 days old when it appears and lists long US equity positions only. It omits the funds' short positions and hedges, credit holdings, derivatives other than listed options, and cash. A third-party summary of the Q3 filing also says some positions were withheld under a confidential-treatment request; any such holdings could appear in a later amendment. Options are shown at notional value. Because most of the book is in two affiliated companies, the 13F says little about broad stock selection, and simply copying it means concentrated exposure to IEP and refining.
