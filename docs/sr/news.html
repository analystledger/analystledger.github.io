Analog Devices beats on 42% growth, yet shares sink ~17% on softer guide and capex jump

Analog Devices posted record fiscal Q4 revenue of $4.37 billion, up 42% from a year earlier and ahead of the roughly $4.32 billion consensus, with adjusted EPS of $4.06 versus about $3.89 expected and up 80% year on year. It guided fiscal Q1 revenue to $4.4 billion ±$150 million but adjusted EPS to $3.82 ±$0.20, below the quarter just reported, and lifted fiscal 2027 capex to $1.6–1.9 billion from $0.7 billion; shares fell about 17–18% premarket and were down roughly 19% by midday.

Zašto je važno: The reaction is a read-through for analog and mixed-signal peers such as Texas Instruments, Microchip and NXP, where investors are weighing strong industrial and data-center demand against heavier spending and easing gross margins. Management said bookings still run above billings, so the open question is whether the capex build and the guided dip to about 74% gross margin prove temporary.

Sanofi bets $41 billion on Insmed, paying a 46% premium in all-cash biotech deal

Sanofi agreed to buy Insmed for $175 per share in cash, valuing the drugmaker at about $41.0 billion and paying roughly 46% above its Nov. 18 close, the last price before deal reports surfaced. Sanofi will fund the purchase with debt and cash, expects it to add to business EPS by the end of 2028, and leaves its 2026 guidance unchanged; Insmed shares rose about 14% before Tuesday's open.

Zašto je važno: The deal shows big pharma paying up for late-stage growth as blockbusters such as Sanofi's Dupixent approach patent expiry, which keeps attention on other mid-cap biotechs. Closing, targeted for the first half of 2027, still needs Insmed shareholder approval and US and international antitrust clearance.
