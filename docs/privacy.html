Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Analyst Ledger is a static website. We do not run accounts, logins, advertising trackers or analytics cookies, and we do not collect personal data from visitors.

What we store

We publish information about professional analysts' publicly reported rating changes (name, firm, date, rating, price target and source link) as reported by the press and data providers.

Our automated posting apps

Our posting integrations (TikTok, Meta, Bluesky, Mastodon, Telegram) are used only to publish to accounts owned by Analyst Ledger. The access tokens for those accounts are stored encrypted and are used only to publish posts. We do not collect, store or share data about other users of those platforms.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses as described in GitHub's own privacy statement.

Contact

Questions or removal requests: open an issue at github.com/analystledger/analystledger.github.io.
