Chipmakers give back Monday's surge as Nvidia drops 7–9% and Nasdaq slides about 2%

A day after Nvidia jumped about 31% on Jensen Huang's reinstatement as CEO, the stock fell roughly 7–9% intraday Tuesday, AMD lost about 9%, Micron and SK Hynix dropped more than 10% and SanDisk, Seagate and Western Digital each fell 5% or more. Analog Devices beats on 42% growth, yet shares sink ~17% on softer guide and capex jump

Analog Devices posted record fiscal Q4 revenue of $4.37 billion, up 42% from a year earlier and ahead of the roughly $4.32 billion consensus, with adjusted EPS of $4.06 versus about $3.89 expected and up 80% year on year. It guided fiscal Q1 revenue to $4.4 billion ±$150 million but adjusted EPS to $3.82 ±$0.20, below the quarter just reported, and lifted fiscal 2027 capex to $1.6–1.9 billion from $0.7 billion; shares fell about 17–18% premarket and were down roughly 19% by midday.

Warum es wichtig ist: The reaction is a read-through for analog and mixed-signal peers such as Texas Instruments, Microchip and NXP, where investors are weighing strong industrial and data-center demand against heavier spending and easing gross margins. Management said bookings still run above billings, so the open question is whether the capex build and the guided dip to about 74% gross margin prove temporary.

Dick's jumps ~16% as Q3 profit tops forecasts and full-year EPS outlook moves higher

Dick's Sporting Goods reported Q3 net sales up 23% to $5.13 billion, helped by a full quarter of Foot Locker, and adjusted EPS of $1.94 against a consensus near $1.41, with comparable sales up 2.6% at the core Dick's business and 0.5% at Foot Locker. It raised its 2026 adjusted EPS outlook to $11.45–$12.30 from $11.00–$12.00, bought back $903 million of stock in the quarter, and its shares rose about 16% Tuesday morning.

Warum es wichtig ist: The results inform the read on athletic retail and brand partners such as Nike, as Foot Locker posts its first slight comp gain under new ownership. Foot Locker remains loss-making, with 134 stores closed so far this year and up to $750 million in total restructuring charges planned.

Best Buy posts its strongest comps in five years at +6.1% and lifts full-year outlook

Best Buy's fiscal Q3 revenue rose 5.8% to $10.24 billion and comparable sales grew 6.1%, well above the roughly 2.3% analysts expected, while adjusted EPS of $1.62 beat the $1.49 consensus and rose from $1.40 a year earlier. Kohl's returns to sales growth and tops EPS forecasts, helped by one-time gains

Kohl's reported Q3 comparable sales up 1.2%, its first positive quarterly comp in a long stretch of declines, and adjusted EPS of $1.76 against Wall Street expectations near breakeven, with gross margin up 145 basis points to 41.0%. Sanofi bets $41 billion on Insmed, paying a 46% premium in all-cash biotech deal

Sanofi agreed to buy Insmed for $175 per share in cash, valuing the drugmaker at about $41.0 billion and paying roughly 46% above its Nov. 18 close, the last price before deal reports surfaced. Sanofi will fund the purchase with debt and cash, expects it to add to business EPS by the end of 2028, and leaves its 2026 guidance unchanged; Insmed shares rose about 14% before Tuesday's open.

Warum es wichtig ist: The deal shows big pharma paying up for late-stage growth as blockbusters such as Sanofi's Dupixent approach patent expiry, which keeps attention on other mid-cap biotechs. Closing, targeted for the first half of 2027, still needs Insmed shareholder approval and US and international antitrust clearance.

HPE beats and raises, yet shares slide as AI server boom threatens margins

Shares still fell roughly 10% to 12% before Tuesday's open after management said its Cloud & AI segment margin could ease to about 14% next year from 18.4% in Q4 as lower-margin AI systems make up more of sales.

Warum es wichtig ist: The reaction puts AI hardware profitability, not just demand, under the spotlight, a question that also hangs over server peers such as Dell and Super Micro. How deep the squeeze runs depends on the timing and mix of AI system shipments, which HPE expects to tilt toward the second half of fiscal 2027.
