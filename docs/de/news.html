Dollar Tree jumps premarket after Q3 beat and higher full-year profit outlook

Dollar Tree posted Q3 net sales of $5.07 billion, up 6.9%, with comparable sales up 4.6% on higher traffic, and adjusted EPS of $1.16 versus a $0.91 consensus. It raised its full-year adjusted EPS outlook to $8.05-$8.25, which includes about $0.65 from tariff refunds, and shares rose about 11% premarket.

Warum es wichtig ist: Traffic gains are a read-through on lower-income consumer spending heading into the holidays, relevant to other discount retailers. Part of the raised outlook relies on tariff refunds rather than operations.

Sanofi agrees to buy Insmed for $175 a share in cash, about $41 billion

Insmed said Sanofi will acquire it for $175 per share in cash, an equity value of roughly $41.0 billion and about a 46% premium to the Nov. 18 unaffected close. Both boards approved the deal, which is expected to close in the first half of 2027 and be funded with debt and cash.

Warum es wichtig ist: The deal sets a cash value for Insmed shares and is one of the largest biotech takeovers of the year. Closing still depends on Insmed shareholder approval and US and non-US regulatory clearances.

$INSM $SNY

Quellen: Insmed press release (SEC 8-K), Investing.com

Hoher Einfluss 2026-11-23 21:05 UTC · Quartalszahlen

HPE falls premarket despite Q4 beat as it flags AI server margin pressure

Hewlett Packard Enterprise reported fiscal Q4 revenue of $14.5 billion, up 50%, and non-GAAP EPS of $1.43, raised its fiscal 2027 outlook to $5.05-$5.35 EPS and added a $5 billion buyback. Shares still fell roughly 10% or more before Tuesday's open after the CFO warned growing AI server sales could weigh on margins later in fiscal 2027.

Warum es wichtig ist: The reaction highlights investor focus on profitability of AI hardware, which could matter for other server makers. How much margins compress depends on the mix of AI systems shipped in the second half.

$HPE

Quellen: HPE press release (SEC 8-K), Seeking Alpha, Investing.com

Agilent rises premarket after fiscal Q4 revenue and profit top estimates

Agilent reported fiscal Q4 revenue of $2.04 billion against a $1.99 billion consensus and adjusted EPS of $1.86 versus $1.73 expected, with adjusted operating margin up 280 basis points to 30%. Shares were up about 5.7% in premarket trading Tuesday.

Warum es wichtig ist: The results are a data point on lab and life-science tools demand, which also affects peers in the group. Whether premarket gains hold through the regular session is uncertain in a volatile market.

Tesla jumps about 12% on SpaceX merger reports, HSBC upgrade and market rebound

Tesla rose as much as 13.9% on Monday as Bloomberg reported a possible Tesla-SpaceX merger could come as soon as December, and HSBC raised the stock to hold from reduce with a $359 target, up from $157. The move came during a broad tech rally and after the weekend rollout of FSD version 15.

Warum es wichtig ist: A combination with SpaceX would reshape Tesla's valuation and shareholder base, but no merger has been officially announced and terms, timing and approvals are unknown.

Nvidia reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO; shares jump about 30%

Nvidia's board brought co-founder Jensen Huang back as president, CEO and director with immediate effect, 11 days after he resigned. The stock opened up about 33%, its largest intraday gain since 2003, and lifted chipmakers and AI-infrastructure names including neocloud providers.

Arm extends CEO Rene Haas's contract through 2033; shares climb about 16%

Arm said Rene Haas will keep leading the chip designer through 2033 and will also become vice chairman of majority owner SoftBank Group from December 1. Earlier this month Nvidia's board had reportedly approached Haas as a possible successor to Jensen Huang.

CrowdStrike agrees to buy Zscaler in all-stock deal worth about $43.9 billion

CrowdStrike will pay 1.135 of its shares for each Zscaler share, about a 34% premium to Zscaler's previous close. Zscaler rose sharply before the open while CrowdStrike fell about 9%, as investors weighed dilution for the buyer.

Warum es wichtig ist: Creates one of the largest pure-play cybersecurity companies and a stronger rival to Palo Alto Networks. The deal still needs shareholder and regulatory approval.
