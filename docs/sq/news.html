Brokers race to let AI agents trade for clients: Interactive Brokers, Robinhood and eToro all go live

Interactive Brokers launched IBKR Autonomous Trading on 21 October 2026: eligible clients can authorise a connected AI to scan more than 75,000 listed stocks, options and ETFs and submit supported orders without approving each one, inside a separate 'AI Partition' funded from their main account. Robinhood rolled out non-technical AI trading agents to its roughly 29 million users at its HOOD Summit on 29 September, and eToro launched connectors for Claude, ChatGPT and Grok on 8 October; in its 10 November Q3 results eToro said users engaged in API and agentic trading rose 250% from Q2 (secondary source).

Pse ka rëndësi: Agent trading could lift trading volumes and client activity — the main revenue drivers for IBKR, HOOD and ETOR — but liability for agent mistakes is still unsettled and FINRA has flagged autonomous agents as needing new supervision. All three firms ring-fence the agent in a separate sub-account with its own funding limit, which caps losses but not the risk of a bad strategy.

Deere guides 2027 profit to $4.5-5.25 billion, below estimates, as large-ag sales sink 15%

Deere earned $1.051 billion, or $3.89 a share, in its fiscal fourth quarter, below the roughly $4.05 analysts expected (secondary source), as total net sales and revenues slipped 2% to $12.1 billion and Production & Precision Ag sales fell 15% with segment profit down to $406 million from $604 million. HP beats its own Q4 guide on AI PCs, but memory costs keep FY27 outlook cautious
