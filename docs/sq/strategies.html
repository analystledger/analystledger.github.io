The strategies

Dollar-cost averaging (DCA)

low risk · checks monthly
Used by
Pionex and 3Commas DCA bots; Binance Auto-Invest; recurring buys at most brokers
Rules
Buy the same amount of a broad index ETF (here SPY) on the first trading day of every month, whatever the price.
Good for
Regular savers; removes the stress of picking the moment.
Main risk
Falls with the market; in a rising market investing a lump sum early usually ends higher.

Rebalancing bot (60/40)

low risk · checks monthly
Used by
Pionex and 3Commas rebalancing bots; Robinhood agents can rebalance a portfolio
Rules
Hold 60% stocks (SPY) and 40% bonds (AGG). Once a month, sell what has grown above its share and buy what has fallen below it.
Good for
Smoother ride than all-stocks; automatically sells high and buys low.
Main risk
Bonds and stocks can fall together, as in 2022.
Backtest
9.4% a year, worst fall -21.7%

Trend filter (200-day average)

low risk · checks daily
Used by
Trend filters inside Composer symphonies and most rule-based bot builders
Rules
Own SPY while it closes above its 200-day moving average; switch to T-bills (BIL) when it closes below, and back when it recovers.
Good for
Cutting losses in long bear markets.
Main risk
Whipsaws: several small losses when the price hovers around the average; misses the first part of rebounds.
Backtest
10.7% a year, worst fall -20.4%

Moving-average crossover (50/200)

medium risk · checks daily
Used by
Signal bots on 3Commas and TradingView alerts; classic 'golden cross' rule
Rules
Own SPY while its 50-day average is above its 200-day average; hold T-bills when it is below.
Good for
Very few trades (about one a year).
Main risk
Slow: reacts weeks after a fast crash such as 2020.
Backtest
10.0% a year, worst fall -33.7%

Momentum rotation

medium risk · checks monthly
Used by
Composer symphonies (e.g. 'Dual Momentum with Fallback', 'AI Flywheel Momentum Harvester')
Rules
Once a month, rank SPY, QQQ, EFA and GLD by their 6-month return. Hold the top two (50% each); any of the two with a negative return is replaced by bonds (AGG).
Good for
Riding whichever market is strongest.
Main risk
Sharp reversals hurt: it can buy what just peaked.
Backtest
12.4% a year, worst fall -30.9%

Dual momentum with bond fallback

medium risk · checks monthly
Used by
Composer 'Dual Momentum' symphonies; a widely copied academic rule
Rules
Monthly: if SPY's 12-month return beats T-bills, hold the better of US (SPY) and international (EFA) stocks; otherwise hold bonds (AGG).
Good for
Long bear markets such as 2008.
Main risk
Slow 12-month signal; lagged badly in the fast 2020 crash and rebound.
Backtest
8.2% a year, worst fall -33.7%

RSI mean reversion (buy the dip)

medium risk · checks daily
Used by
RSI rules in Composer symphonies, TradingView and signal bots
Rules
When SPY is above its 200-day average and its 2-day RSI falls below 10, buy at the close; sell when it closes above its 5-day average. Otherwise sit in T-bills.
Good for
Short, frequent trades with little time in the market.
Main risk
Each dip can keep falling; works only in uptrends; many small trades.
Backtest
4.9% a year, worst fall -10.9%

Volatility switch (VIX)

low risk · checks daily
Used by
eToro Agent Portfolios example agent (VIX above 25 = defensive, below 20 = back in)
Rules
Hold SPY. When the VIX closes above 25, switch to bonds (AGG); return to SPY when the VIX closes below 20.
Good for
Stepping aside during panics.
Main risk
Often sells after the drop and buys back after the rebound; did not help in 2022.
Backtest
5.7% a year, worst fall -29.4%

Crash breaker (T-bills + gold)

medium risk · checks daily
Used by
Composer symphonies ('if SPY drops more than 3% in 10 days, switch to T-bills and gold')
Rules
Hold SPY. If SPY has fallen more than 3% over the last 10 trading days, hold 50% T-bills (BIL) and 50% gold (GLD) instead until it recovers.
Good for
Short, sharp sell-offs.
Main risk
Many false alarms; switching costs add up.
Backtest
9.1% a year, worst fall -35.3%

Grid bot

medium risk · checks daily
Used by
Pionex, 3Commas and Bitsgap grid bots (built for crypto; shown here on SPY)
Rules
Start 50% invested in SPY. Every 2% fall buys another 10% of capital; every 2% rise sells 10%.
Good for
Sideways, choppy markets.
Main risk
Sells too early in strong uptrends; buys all the way down in a crash.
Backtest
5.8% a year, worst fall -16.8%

Weekly covered call

medium risk · checks weekly · uses options
Used by
Options income bots and AI agents at IBKR, Robinhood and Composer; covered-call ETFs
Rules
Own 100 shares; each week sell one call about 1 sigma above the price; buy it back at 50% profit; skip weeks with earnings or a Fed decision.
Good for
Steady income on shares you would hold anyway.
Main risk
Keeps all the downside of the shares but caps the upside.
Backtest
11.5% a year, worst fall -31.2% (modelled)

Weekly cash-secured put

medium risk · checks weekly · uses options
Used by
Options income bots and AI agents at IBKR and Robinhood; put-write indexes
Rules
Hold cash in T-bills; each week sell one put about 1 sigma below the price, covered by cash; buy it back at 50% profit; skip event weeks.
Good for
Getting paid while waiting to buy lower.
Main risk
A crash week can wipe out months of premium.
Backtest
5.3% a year, worst fall -19.5% (modelled)

AI drawdown watch

low risk · checks daily
Used by
IBKR Autonomous Trading example ('if a position falls more than 7% from its 20-day high, send a brief')
Rules
No trades. Every day, check each holding; if one falls more than 7% from its 20-day high, send a short note on the cause and current option prices.
Good for
Staying informed without watching screens.
Main risk
Information only; you still decide.

Left out on purpose

  • Martingale bots (double the stake after every loss): one long losing run empties the account.
  • Leveraged and inverse ETFs, leveraged grids, margin: losses compound faster than gains; common in high-return Composer symphonies.
  • Crypto bots and arbitrage: outside this site's stock focus and far more volatile.
