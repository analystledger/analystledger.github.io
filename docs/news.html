High impact 2026-11-24 11:30 UTC · Earnings

Dollar Tree shoppers keep coming: traffic-led Q3 beat lifts full-year outlook

Dollar Tree's Q3 comparable sales rose 4.6% as store visits climbed 3.8%, lifting net sales 6.9% to $5.07 billion, and adjusted EPS of $1.16 cleared the $0.91 consensus despite a $0.42 hit from tariff-related reinvestment. The retailer raised its full-year adjusted EPS outlook to $8.05-$8.25, including about $0.65 from tariff refunds, and shares jumped about 11% in premarket trading.

Why it matters: Rising traffic at a discount chain is an early read on how budget-conscious households are spending into the holiday season, relevant for peers like Dollar General and Five Below. Part of the higher outlook rests on one-time tariff refunds, so the underlying earnings trend is less clear than the headline raise suggests.

$DLTR

Sources: Dollar Tree press release (SEC 8-K), Investing.com

High impact 2026-11-24 11:00 UTC · Mergers & acquisitions

Sanofi bets $41 billion on Insmed, paying a 46% premium in all-cash biotech deal

Sanofi agreed to buy Insmed for $175 per share in cash, valuing the drugmaker at about $41.0 billion and paying roughly 46% above its Nov. 18 close, the last price before deal reports surfaced. Sanofi will fund the purchase with debt and cash, expects it to add to business EPS by the end of 2028, and leaves its 2026 guidance unchanged; Insmed shares rose about 14% before Tuesday's open.

Why it matters: The deal shows big pharma paying up for late-stage growth as blockbusters such as Sanofi's Dupixent approach patent expiry, which keeps attention on other mid-cap biotechs. Closing, targeted for the first half of 2027, still needs Insmed shareholder approval and US and international antitrust clearance.

$INSM $SNY

Sources: Insmed press release (SEC 8-K), Investing.com

High impact 2026-11-23 21:05 UTC · Earnings

HPE beats and raises, yet shares slide as AI server boom threatens margins

Hewlett Packard Enterprise grew fiscal Q4 revenue 50% to $14.5 billion, earned $1.43 a share on a non-GAAP basis against its own $1.20-$1.30 forecast, lifted its fiscal 2027 outlook to $5.05-$5.35 per share and added $5 billion to its buyback. Shares still fell roughly 10% to 12% before Tuesday's open after management said its Cloud & AI segment margin could ease to about 14% next year from 18.4% in Q4 as lower-margin AI systems make up more of sales.

Why it matters: The reaction puts AI hardware profitability, not just demand, under the spotlight, a question that also hangs over server peers such as Dell and Super Micro. How deep the squeeze runs depends on the timing and mix of AI system shipments, which HPE expects to tilt toward the second half of fiscal 2027.

$HPE

Sources: HPE press release (SEC 8-K), Seeking Alpha, Investing.com

Medium impact 2026-11-23 21:05 UTC · Earnings

Agilent tops estimates with a 30% operating margin, sending shares higher premarket

Agilent's fiscal Q4 revenue reached $2.04 billion against a $1.99 billion consensus, and adjusted EPS of $1.86 beat the $1.73 estimate. Adjusted operating margin widened 280 basis points to 30%, and shares rose about 5.7% before Tuesday's open.

Why it matters: Agilent is a bellwether for lab-instrument and life-science tools spending by drugmakers and research labs, so its results are watched across the sector. Whether the early gain holds is uncertain on a day when tech-heavy futures were pointing lower.

$A

Sources: Investing.com

US stocks rebound sharply as chip and AI shares lead after last week's rout

By midday Monday the Nasdaq Composite was up about 6.7% and the S&P 500 about 4.9%, with the Philadelphia chip index up roughly 13% and every sector but energy higher. The bounce follows a week in which the S&P 500 fell 8.6% and confirmed a correction.

High impact 2026-11-23 14:30 UTC · Other

Tesla jumps about 12% on SpaceX merger reports, HSBC upgrade and market rebound

Tesla rose as much as 13.9% on Monday as Bloomberg reported a possible Tesla-SpaceX merger could come as soon as December, and HSBC raised the stock to hold from reduce with a $359 target, up from $157. The move came during a broad tech rally and after the weekend rollout of FSD version 15.

Why it matters: A combination with SpaceX would reshape Tesla's valuation and shareholder base, but no merger has been officially announced and terms, timing and approvals are unknown.

High impact 2026-11-23 14:30 UTC · Leadership

Nvidia reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO; shares jump about 30%

Nvidia's board brought co-founder Jensen Huang back as president, CEO and director with immediate effect, 11 days after he resigned. The stock opened up about 33%, its largest intraday gain since 2003, and lifted chipmakers and AI-infrastructure names including neocloud providers.

Why it matters: Removes the leadership uncertainty behind November's AI selloff. An SEC inquiry, shareholder lawsuits and national-security questions remain open.

High impact 2026-11-23 12:49 UTC · Leadership

Arm extends CEO Rene Haas's contract through 2033; shares climb about 16%

Arm said Rene Haas will keep leading the chip designer through 2033 and will also become vice chairman of majority owner SoftBank Group from December 1. Earlier this month Nvidia's board had reportedly approached Haas as a possible successor to Jensen Huang.

Why it matters: The extension settles leadership questions at Arm and ties Haas more closely to SoftBank. How his larger SoftBank role will split his time is not yet clear.

$ARM $NVDA

Sources: Reuters via Investing.com, Yahoo Finance

High impact 2026-11-23 10:39 UTC · Mergers & acquisitions

CrowdStrike agrees to buy Zscaler in all-stock deal worth about $43.9 billion

CrowdStrike will pay 1.135 of its shares for each Zscaler share, about a 34% premium to Zscaler's previous close. Zscaler rose sharply before the open while CrowdStrike fell about 9%, as investors weighed dilution for the buyer.

Why it matters: Creates one of the largest pure-play cybersecurity companies and a stronger rival to Palo Alto Networks. The deal still needs shareholder and regulatory approval.
