Budget & strategy planner

Tell the planner your budget and how much risk you accept. It shows which common stock and options strategies your budget can support, the most you could lose, and what analysts tracked on {{ CFG.site_name }} say about a stock. It is an educational calculator, not personal financial advice.

Private: everything is calculated in your browser. Nothing you type is sent or stored.

The planner applies simple, published rules of thumb (position sizing, the cash that each strategy requires, its worst case) to the numbers you enter. It does not know your full finances, taxes or goals and does not recommend buying or selling any security. Prices may be delayed.
